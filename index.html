Ready to danceAUTO · -- BPM

For imported music, Auto responds to detected transients. For exact choreography, switch to BPM lock, enter the song tempo or tap the beat. The studio beat machine also drives dancers directly.

In Dance Preview, characters animate silently at the chosen BPM so you can test every move before loading music. Tap Edit beside a saved dancer, make changes, then Save selected. For a fresh routine tap New moves now.

The 2D and 3D modes use the same saved characters. 3D is rendered with WebGL on your device, without cloud processing or a paid account. Your custom characters overlap the entire GrooveLab page. Drag them anywhere, on mobile too. On desktop Chrome, use Pop-out for a floating illustrated mini-window; full 3D remains inside GrooveLab. On iPhone, the Pop-out button offers a video Picture-in-Picture fallback when supported; it is a loop of Nova, not your customized character.
